Welcome

Select structures on the left, then tap a label on the body.
Each structure card shows its Latin name, location in the body systems, key anatomy, a clinical pearl and a memory aid where available. Use Recall to hide names and test yourself, or Quiz to identify and locate structures.

How to study

  1. Pick a quick set (e.g. Heart detail, ECG leads).
  2. Stand back so your whole body is in view, or use the mannequin.
  3. Tap any label to read the card; Recall → tap “#n · ?” to reveal.
  4. Finish with Quiz → Identify, then Locate.
